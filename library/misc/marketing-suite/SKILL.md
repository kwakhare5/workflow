# Marketing Suite — Full Growth & Acquisition Engine

This suite consolidates growth strategy, copy, offers, funnel optimization, and distribution.

## Core Commands & Sub-modules

1. **/marketing-plan & /marketing-council**: Stage-tailored growth roadmaps, channel prioritization, and legendary advisor panels.
   - Reference: `references/marketing-plan.md`, `references/marketing-council.md`
2. **/copywriting & /no-ai-slop**: High-converting landing page headlines, hooks, value props, and anti-slop editing.
   - Reference: `references/copywriting.md`, `references/content-strategy.md`
3. **/offers & /pricing**: Value equation packaging, pricing tiers, risk-reversal guarantees, and bonuses.
   - Reference: `references/offers.md`, `references/pricing.md`
4. **/cro & /ab-testing**: Landing page and onboarding funnel conversion rate optimization, exit popups, and signup flow audits.
   - Reference: `references/cro.md`, `references/ab-testing.md`, `references/popups.md`
5. **/launch**: GTM release plans, Product Hunt launch checklist, teaser sequences, and launch assets.
   - Reference: `references/launch.md`, `references/product-marketing.md`
6. **/cold-email & /referrals**: Outbound B2B sequences, prospecting, viral referral loops, and affiliate incentives.
   - Reference: `references/cold-email.md`, `references/referrals.md`
7. **/seo & /ai-seo**: SERP optimization, schema markup, and AI engine search discoverability (Perplexity/ChatGPT).
   - Reference: `references/seo-audit.md`, `references/ai-seo.md`, `references/schema.md`

## Workflow
When invoked, identify the specific marketing challenge, view the relevant reference file in `references/`, and execute the strategy.
