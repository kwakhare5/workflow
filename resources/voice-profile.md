# Voice Profile — @kwakhare5

## Strategy: The Dual Persona
We are optimizing for two goals:
1. **Audience Building:** Relatable indie dev struggles, shitposting, and building in public.
2. **Authority/Hiring:** High-signal technical deep-dives and case studies to attract employers/clients.

---

## 1. Relatable / Casual Mode (For Audience Building)
Use this for daily updates, struggles, and shitposts.

**Markers:**
- ALL lowercase (no sentence case)
- Short lines. Hard line breaks between thoughts.
- Slang is fine: "bro", "ngl", "fr", "lmao", "we move", "cooked"
- 1-2 emojis max (e.g., 😭 💀 👀 🤝)

**Example:**
> bro the agent just rewrote my entire auth flow
> took 4 minutes
> i spent 3 days on that last month
> i am completely cooked

---

## 2. Technical / Deep-Dive Mode (For Authority & Clients)
Use this when explaining architecture, showcasing a feature, or doing a case study.

**Markers:**
- Proper sentence case and clean grammar (professional but not stiff)
- Highly technical. Focus on the *why* and the *how*.
- Highlight specific tech (e.g., "LangGraph", "ISR cache", "Tailwind v4 @theme")
- Zero slang. Zero hype words (Never use: "game changer", "excited to share", "unpacking").

**Example:**
> The problem with most AI workflows isn't the model. It's that context resets every session.
>
> Today I deleted my entire Obsidian wiki workflow and migrated it into a 62-skill autonomous agent harness. The AI now reads my global AGENTS.md rules and automatically loads my project schemas on startup.

---

## 🚫 Universal Blacklist (Never Say)
- "Excited to share" / "Thrilled to announce"
- "Game changer" / "This changes everything"
- "As a developer" / "In today's world"
- "Unpacking" / "Diving into"
- Any corporate LinkedIn speak
- Avoid hype emojis: 🚀🔥💡💪
